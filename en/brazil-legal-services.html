Before you contact us

Will I need to travel to Brazil?

That depends on the steps required in your matter. We assess which can be handled remotely or through a representative and explain whether any require your personal attendance.

Can you help with a property in Brazil?

We advise on contracts, title and ownership documentation, leases, and inheritance matters. Where brokerage or practical local support is needed, the responsibilities of independent professionals are agreed separately.

How do I get started?

Tell us where you live, what you need help with, where the property or proceedings are located, and whether there is a deadline. We will then explain the documents needed, proposed scope, and fees.

Tell us about your matter.

Send us a brief summary so we can determine whether we are able to assist.
