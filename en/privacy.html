Privacy notice

Information submitted through the contact form

Information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your enquiry and assess whether we can assist with your legal matter. Please do not submit passwords, banking details or confidential documents through the initial contact form.

The contact form uses FormSubmit to process your enquiry and forward it to the firm's email address. The details you submit are shared with that service for this purpose. WhatsApp is available as a separate contact option. Submitting information does not retain the firm, establish an attorney-client relationship or guarantee that we will accept the matter.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. Service providers needed for communication and website operations may process information for those purposes.

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to measure website visits, pages viewed and interactions, including enquiries initiated through the contact form. Analytics does not receive the contents of the form. Declining analytics cookies does not prevent you from using the website. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

To request information about your personal data, or ask for correction or deletion, contact contato@cmsm.com.br. Information may be retained where required or permitted by law.
